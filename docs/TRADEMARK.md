## 🌟 Introduction

This Trademark Policy ("Policy") governs the use of all trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, and other distinctive brand features (collectively, "TextIt Trademarks") owned by TextIt Corporation ("we," "us," or "our"). This Policy applies to all individuals and entities who wish to use TextIt Trademarks in connection with TextIt 3.0 and related services.

In addition to registered trademarks, TextIt Corporation claims common law rights in:

- **TextIt CLI**

### 2.4 Trademark Symbols

When using TextIt Trademarks, please include the appropriate symbol (® or ™) on first or most prominent use in each piece of content. For example:

- **TextIt® CLI** (first use)
- **TextIt CLI** (subsequent uses)
- **TextIt™ Command** (for unregistered marks)

### 2.5 Version-Specific Branding

When referring to specific versions of TextIt, always include the version number in the following format:
- ✅ **Correct**: TextIt 3.0
- ❌ **Incorrect**: TextIt v3.0 or TextIt v3

## ✅ Permitted Uses

### 3.1 Open Source Projects

You may use TextIt Trademarks to refer to the TextIt software in accordance with the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, provided that:

- Your use is truthful and not misleading
- You include proper attribution
- You do not suggest endorsement or sponsorship by TextIt Corporation
- You follow the guidelines in this Policy

### 3.2 Software Development

You may use TextIt Trademarks to indicate compatibility or interoperability with TextIt software, provided that:

1. Your use is limited to statements like "Works with TextIt" or "Compatible with TextIt"
2. You do not use TextIt Trademarks in your product or company name
3. You include appropriate disclaimers (see Section 7)

### 3.3 Community and Educational Use

You may use TextIt Trademarks in:

- Educational materials and presentations (with proper attribution)
- Community forums and discussions (when discussing TextIt)
- Non-commercial blogs and social media (with #TextItCLI hashtag)
- Local user group meetings and hackathons
- Technical documentation and tutorials

## 🚫 Prohibited Uses

### 4.1 General Prohibitions

You may not use TextIt Trademarks in any manner that:

1. Is likely to cause confusion about the source of goods or services
2. Falsely implies a relationship with or endorsement by TextIt Corporation
3. Disparages or tarnishes the TextIt brand
4. Violates any applicable laws or regulations

### 4.2 Specific Prohibitions

Without prior written permission, you may not:

1. Use TextIt Trademarks in your company, product, or service name
2. Register or use domain names containing TextIt Trademarks
3. Use TextIt logos as your own or as part of your branding
4. Create or distribute merchandise featuring TextIt Trademarks
5. Use TextIt Trademarks in paid advertising without approval

## 🎨 Logo Usage Guidelines

### 5.1 Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the TextIt logo equal to at least 150% of the logo's height on all sides. For the TextIt CLI logo, maintain clear space equal to the height of the terminal icon.

### 5.2 Minimum Size

The minimum size for the TextIt logo is 24px in height for digital use and 0.5 inches for print. For the TextIt CLI logo, the minimum size is 32px in height to ensure legibility of the terminal icon.

### 5.3 Color Variations

Use only the official color variations:

- **Full Color**: Primary logo with brand colors
- **Black**: For dark backgrounds
- **White**: For colored or dark backgrounds
- **Monochrome**: For single-color applications

## 🔍 Fair Use Policy

### 6.1 Nominative Use

You may use TextIt Trademarks to refer to our products and services, provided that:

- You do not suggest endorsement or sponsorship
- You use the minimum necessary to identify the product
- You include a disclaimer such as: "TextIt® is a registered trademark of TextIt Corporation. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by TextIt Corporation."

### 6.2 Comparative Advertising

Comparative advertising using TextIt Trademarks must:

1. Be truthful and not misleading
2. Clearly identify TextIt as the owner of the marks
3. Include appropriate disclaimers

## ™️ Trademark Notices

### 7.1 General Attribution

Include the following notice on materials featuring TextIt Trademarks:

> "TextIt® is a registered trademark of TextIt Corporation. All rights reserved."

### 7.2 Website Footer

For websites referencing TextIt, include in the footer:

> "TextIt® and the TextIt logo are registered trademarks of TextIt Corporation. [Your Company Name] is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by TextIt Corporation."

### 7.3 Product Packaging

For physical products compatible with TextIt:

> "Compatible with TextIt®. TextIt® is a registered trademark of TextIt Corporation. [Your Product] is not manufactured by, affiliated with, or endorsed by TextIt Corporation."

## 🌐 Domain Names & Social Media

### 8.1 Domain Names

You may not register or use domain names containing TextIt Trademarks without prior written permission. This includes:

- textit[anything].com/net/org/io/dev
- text-it[anything].tld
- [anything]textit.tld
- textitcli[anything].tld
- textit3[anything].tld

### 8.2 Social Media Handles

Do not register social media accounts using TextIt Trademarks in a way that suggests official affiliation, such as:
- @TextItSupport
- @TextItHelp
- @TextItOfficial
